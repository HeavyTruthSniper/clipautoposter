Clip AutoPoster

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

Clip AutoPoster is a Windows app made by Brandon Builds Co. It posts gaming clips from your PC to the video platforms you connect. This policy explains what the app handles and where it goes.

What stays on your PC

  • Your clips, and the vertical copies the app makes of them.
  • The titles it writes and its record of what it has posted.
  • The sign-in tokens each platform gives the app when you connect your account.

We don't receive, store or see any of this. The app has no servers of its own that your videos pass through.

What goes to the platforms you connect

When the app posts a clip, it sends the video, its title and its description straight from your PC to that platform, using the sign-in you approved. Each platform handles that data under its own privacy policy:

  • YouTube: Google Privacy Policy
  • TikTok: TikTok Privacy Policy

Sign-in and permissions

You connect each account through that platform's own sign-in page. The app asks only for the permissions it needs to upload and post your videos and to show which account is connected. You can disconnect it at any time from your account settings on each platform, or by deleting the app's token files on your PC.

Google user data

Clip AutoPoster's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data.
  • We don't use your videos or account data for advertising.
  • We don't collect analytics about how you use the app.

If you contact us or buy Pro

If you email us, we keep your email address and message to reply to you. If you buy Pro, the payment provider processes your payment, and we keep your email address and license record to deliver and support your license.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
