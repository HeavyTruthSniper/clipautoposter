How it works

Set it up once. After that, every clip you save goes out on its own, sized for vertical screens and titled from what happened in it.

  1. Save a clip

    Clip a play the way you already do. Clip AutoPoster sees the new file in your clip folder within seconds.

  2. It reads the kill feed

    It reads your knocks, kills, weapon and distance off the screen, picks your best clips first, and skips clips with nothing in them.

  3. It posts

    It turns the clip vertical and posts it with its title and your channel line. No two posts get the same title.

Titles it has written from real clips:

  • Triple down with the M24
  • Ran him over with the UAZ
  • Domed him with the Kar98k
  • Winner Winner Chicken Dinner

Questions

Which games does it work with?

Kill-feed titles work with PUBG: Battlegrounds today. Support for more games is planned.

Does it post to TikTok directly?

Yes. You choose who can see each post before anything goes out, and you can turn TikTok off at any time.

What about Instagram Reels?

Reels support is in testing and isn't available yet.

Do you see my videos or my accounts?

No. Clip AutoPoster runs on your PC and signs in through each platform's own login. Your videos go straight from your PC to the platforms you choose. See the privacy policy.
