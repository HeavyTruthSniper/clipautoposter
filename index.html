Clip Autoposter

Clip Autoposter is a personal tool used only by its owner (Brandon Builds Co / Not The Best Gaming) to upload the owner's own gaming clips to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

Privacy Policy

What it accesses: It signs in with each platform's official login (Google sign-in, TikTok Login Kit) and requests only the permissions needed to upload and post videos to the owner's account.

What it collects: Nothing. It does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are sent only to the platform that issued them.

Google API Services: Clip Autoposter's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Terms of Service

Clip Autoposter is provided for the owner's personal use only. It is not offered to the public, and no other users may sign in to it. Content it posts is the owner's own and follows each platform's terms and community guidelines.
